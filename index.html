TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Robots, edge computing, and human interaction

Teams decide how sensing, computation, communication, and decision-making are distributed across the robotic system.

1

Mobile Robot Team

Robots perform sensing, local computation, motion control, and exploration.

  • Onboard sensing
  • Local computation
  • Motion and control
  • Robot-to-robot communication
2

Edge Computing Node

Each team has one local GPU-enabled edge computing node.

  • Compute offloading
  • Multi-robot coordination
  • Map / data fusion
  • Perception and planning
  • LLM inference
3

Human Operator + LLM

Natural-language mission updates are interpreted and translated into executable robot tasks.

  • Mission updates
  • Instruction interpretation
  • Task generation
  • Human-in-the-loop interaction
The key design question: How should computation be distributed between the robots and the edge node while balancing mission performance and energy consumption?

EVALUATION

Each team will perform three mission runs. The final score will be based on average performance across runs.

Mission Performance

Complete the mission

  • Rescue tasks completed
  • Completion time
  • Response to mission updates
Energy Efficiency

Use resources wisely

  • Battery discharge
  • Compute usage
  • Communication usage
  • Energy-aware system design
Final score = mission success balanced by energy / resource efficiency

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply individually?

Yes. Students may apply individually or as a team. Individual applicants may be matched with other participants.

Do I need to have a complete team before applying?

No. Complete teams are welcome, but individual applications are also accepted.

Can our team have a mentor?

Yes. Each team may invite one PhD student or postdoctoral researcher to serve as a mentor.

What resources will be provided?

Each team will have access to mobile robots, a Gazebo-based simulation environment, and a GPU-enabled edge computing node.

Can we use external cloud or API resources?

No external cloud or API access will be available during official competition runs.

How will the competition be scored?

Evaluation will balance mission performance and energy/resource efficiency. Detailed scoring rules will be announced before the kick-off.
