THE CHALLENGE

Intelligent robots.
One shared mission.

The KTH Edge AI Robotics Challenge brings together students from robotics, computer science, communication, and control to develop intelligent multi-robot systems for complex collaborative missions.

Teams will work with multiple mobile robots, an edge computing platform, communication networks, and a human operator supported by large language models. The challenge focuses on how perception, communication, computation, and decision-making can be jointly designed for autonomous robotic systems.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

From human intent
to robot coordination.

Each team operates a fleet of mobile robots connected through a shared communication and edge-computing infrastructure. The edge node supports computationally intensive tasks and coordination, while a human operator can provide high-level mission updates through an LLM-based interface.
