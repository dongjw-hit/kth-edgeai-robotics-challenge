THE CHALLENGE

Each team will control a group of approximately four wheeled mobile robots operating in an unknown indoor environment. During the mission, the robots may need to:

  • Explore an initially unknown environment
  • Detect and identify different targets
  • Share information between robots
  • Allocate tasks among robots
  • Form robot groups to complete specific rescue tasks
  • Adapt their behavior according to mission updates
  • Manage computation, communication, and energy consumption

Some targets may require multiple robots to arrive at the target simultaneously before the rescue task is considered completed.

Arena target: about 5 × 5 m indoor exploration area

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

1. Mobile robot team

  • Robots perform sensing, local computation, motion control, and exploration.
  • Robots can communicate and share local information with each other.

2. Edge computing node

  • A single local computing server is available to each team.
  • Teams decide which tasks are executed onboard and which are offloaded to the edge.
  • Typical edge functions may include multi-robot coordination, perception, map fusion, task allocation, and LLM inference.

3. Human operator + LLM

  • The human operator provides natural-language mission updates.
  • The LLM interprets the instructions and converts them into executable tasks for the robot team.
