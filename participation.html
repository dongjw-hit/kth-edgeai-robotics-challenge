KTH EDGEAI COOPERATIVE ROBOTICS CHALLENGE

Participation

The challenge is designed for student teams with complementary backgrounds in robotics, artificial intelligence, communication, and control. Participants will work together to develop and evaluate a complete cooperative robotic system.

Format

Participants

Master’s students. Applications may be submitted individually or as a team.

Team composition

4–5 students per team. Each team should cover at least three of the following areas: Robotics, CS/AI, Communication, and Control/Autonomous Systems.

Number of teams

3 competing teams.

Mentorship

Each team may be supported by one PhD student or postdoctoral researcher.

Preparation time

Approximately 2 months.

Competition duration

1 day.

Robots

4 four-wheeled mobile robots per team, together with a Gazebo-based simulation environment.

Compute infrastructure

One GPU-enabled edge computing node per team. No external cloud or API access.

Evaluation dimensions

Each team will perform 3 mission runs, and the final score will be determined based on average performance across the runs.

Mission performance

  • Targets detected
  • Rescue tasks completed
  • Completion time
  • Response to mission updates

Energy and resource efficiency

  • Battery discharge
  • Computation usage
  • Communication usage
  • Resource-aware task execution

FINAL SCORE

Mission performance balanced by energy and resource efficiency
